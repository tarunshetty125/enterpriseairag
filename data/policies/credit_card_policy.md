# Credit Card Policy

## Eligibility

Credit card applicants should have stable income, verified identity, and a
credit score of 650 or higher for standard approval. Premium cards require
higher income and strong repayment behaviour.

## Credit Limit

Credit limit recommendations should consider income, current credit utilization,
existing product exposure, and repayment patterns. Customers with high credit
utilization require a conservative limit.

## Travel and Shopping Benefits

Customers with frequent travel or online shopping activity may be suitable for
reward cards when credit risk is low or medium.

## Restrictions

The assistant must not guarantee approval. It may summarize eligibility rules
and cite supporting policy sections.
