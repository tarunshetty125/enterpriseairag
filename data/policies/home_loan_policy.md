# Home Loan Policy

## Eligibility

Applicants must be at least 21 years old and have documented recurring income.
The preferred minimum credit score is 650. Applicants with a credit score below
600 require manual review and may be declined if other risk indicators are
present.

## Debt Capacity

The debt-to-income ratio should generally remain below 1.5 for standard home
loan eligibility. Existing loan exposure, repayment history, and salary
stability must be reviewed before approving a new loan.

## Documentation

Required documents include identity proof, address proof, income proof, bank
statements, property documents, and credit history consent.

## Decisioning

Relationship managers should use approved ML risk outputs and feature store
values as decision support. The policy assistant may explain rules but must not
make final lending decisions.
