# Investment Product Guide

## Mutual Funds

Mutual funds may suit customers with medium to high income, investment-focused
behaviour, and capacity to tolerate market risk. Recommendations must consider
customer suitability and existing obligations.

## SIP

Systematic investment plans may suit customers with regular salary credits and
consistent surplus cash flow. SIP recommendations should be explained using
income stability, savings ratio, and spending behaviour.

## Fixed Deposit

Fixed deposits may suit customers with surplus savings, conservative risk
preference, or high balances. Fixed deposits are often appropriate when a
customer needs predictable returns.

## Suitability

The recommendation engine output remains the source of truth. The AI assistant
may explain why a product fits but must not invent a new recommendation.
