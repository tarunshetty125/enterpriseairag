# Insurance Policy

## Eligibility

Insurance products may be offered to customers with protection needs, medical
spending indicators, dependents, or higher age bands. Eligibility depends on
product-specific underwriting rules.

## Suitability Signals

Medical-heavy spending, family obligations, and long-term financial planning
needs may support an insurance recommendation. Existing coverage should be
checked before recommending a new policy.

## Documentation

Required documents may include identity proof, nominee details, health
declarations, and income proof for high-value coverage.

## Assistant Limits

The assistant may summarize insurance policy rules but must not provide medical
advice or guarantee underwriting approval.
